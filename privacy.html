Privacy Policy / 개인정보처리방침

Exam Training Timer (시험 훈련 타이머) — Last updated: 2026-09-24

English

We do not collect personal data such as your name, email or location.

  • The app does not require an account or login.
  • One value leaves your device: a hashed device identifier (a one-way SHA-256 hash — the original identifier is never sent) is registered with our server so the 7-day free period applies once per device, even after reinstalling. Nothing else is attached to it: no name, no email, no usage data, no training records. Legal basis / purpose: app functionality and preventing abuse of the free period.
  • Your timer settings and training records are stored only on your device. They are never sent anywhere.
  • The app contains no third-party ad networks, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • The app shows cards introducing other apps by the same developer (when you open the app and at the top of Settings). Tapping a card opens that app's store page. The store link only carries a fixed label saying it came from this app (for example utm_source=examtimer) — it contains nothing about you or your device.
  • On Android, the app checks on your device whether those other apps are already installed, only so that it does not show them again. This check stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.
  • The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.
  • If you delete the app, all locally stored data is deleted with it.
